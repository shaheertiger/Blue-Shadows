The Complete Men's Skincare Routine for Every Skin Type

A great haircut and a sharp beard look even better on healthy skin. Here's a simple routine that works — and how to adjust it for your skin type and the seasons.

October 6, 20263 min readBlue Shadows Barber Studio

A fresh haircut and a sharp beard line only go so far if the skin underneath is dry, flaky, oily, or irritated. The good news: men's skincare doesn't need to be complicated. A few basic steps, done consistently, make a visible difference in a few weeks.

The basic routine: three steps

Morning

  1. Cleanse with a gentle face wash. Skip harsh bar soap — it strips the skin.
  2. Moisturize with a lightweight moisturizer suited to your skin type.
  3. Protect with sunscreen (SPF 30 or higher), even in winter and on cloudy days. Many moisturizers include SPF, which makes this a two-step routine.

Evening

  1. Cleanse to remove the day's oil, sweat, and dirt.
  2. Moisturize — a slightly richer one at night is fine, especially in winter.

That's it. Everything else is an optional upgrade.

How to find your skin type

Wash your face with a gentle cleanser, pat dry, and wait about an hour without applying anything. Then check:

  • Oily: shiny all over, especially on the forehead, nose, and chin.
  • Dry: feels tight, rough, or flaky.
  • Combination: oily in the T-zone (forehead, nose, chin), normal or dry on the cheeks.
  • Normal: comfortable, not shiny or tight.
  • Sensitive: often red, itchy, or reactive to new products or shaving.

Adjusting for your skin type

Oily skin

  • Use a foaming or gel cleanser.
  • Choose an oil-free, lightweight moisturizer — don't skip it, or your skin may produce more oil.
  • A gentle exfoliant once or twice a week helps keep pores clear.

Dry skin

  • Use a cream or hydrating cleanser.
  • Choose a richer moisturizer, and apply it to slightly damp skin to lock in moisture.
  • Avoid very hot showers, which pull moisture from the skin.

Combination skin

  • A gentle gel cleanser works for most.
  • Use a lightweight moisturizer all over, with a little extra on dry areas.

Sensitive skin

  • Choose fragrance-free products with short ingredient lists.
  • Introduce one new product at a time, and patch-test first.
  • Be extra gentle when shaving (more below).

Shaving and beard skin

  • Prep with warmth. Shave after a shower, or use a warm towel, to soften the hair.
  • Use a sharp blade and shave with the grain to reduce irritation and ingrown hairs.
  • Moisturize afterward with an alcohol-free aftershave balm.
  • Don't forget the skin under your beard. Beard oil moisturizes both the hair and the skin, and helps prevent itch and flakes.

Adjusting for Canadian seasons

In Ontario, your skin goes through very different conditions across the year:

  • Winter: cold air outside, dry heated air inside. Switch to a richer moisturizer, avoid hot showers, and use lip balm. Our winter beard care guide covers beards in detail.
  • Summer: humidity and sweat. Switch to a lighter moisturizer, wash after workouts, and be consistent with SPF.

Optional upgrades

  • Exfoliation once or twice a week to remove dead skin.
  • Eye cream if you notice puffiness or dark circles.
  • A face mask occasionally to deep-clean pores.
  • A hot towel treatment to open pores, soften skin, and relax.

If you have persistent acne, rashes, or skin concerns, a doctor or dermatologist is the right person to see.
