Men's Hair Colour and Grey Blending in Milton: What to Know Before Your First Colour

More men in Milton are colouring their hair and beards than ever — usually not to look different, just to look like themselves on a good day. Here's how it works.

October 6, 20263 min readBlue Shadows Barber Studio

Ten years ago, men's hair colour was something most guys didn't talk about. Today, it's one of the fastest-growing services in barbershops, and Milton is no exception. Some clients want to cover every grey. Most just want to soften it — to look less tired, more energetic, and more like themselves.

If you've been thinking about it, here's what you need to know.

Full coverage vs. grey blending

Full coverage

Full coverage colours every grey hair to a single, uniform shade. It's the right choice if you have a small amount of grey you want gone completely, or if you're used to the colour of your hair from your twenties. The downside: as hair grows, a visible line of grey roots appears, so it needs regular touch-ups.

Grey blending

Grey blending reduces the contrast between grey and natural hair without eliminating the grey entirely. The result looks like you, just a few years earlier. It's subtle — friends and coworkers notice that you look sharp, but often can't tell why. Because it fades softly, regrowth is far less obvious than with full coverage.

For most men trying colour for the first time, we recommend starting with a blend. You can always go further next time.

Beard colour

Beards often grey faster and less evenly than the hair on your head — the chin and moustache are usually first. A beard colour service can:

  • Even out patchy grey on the chin and moustache.
  • Match your beard to your hair colour.
  • Make a thinner beard look fuller by darkening lighter hairs.

As with hair, natural-looking results come from choosing a shade close to your natural colour — or even a little lighter — rather than going jet black.

Short hair vs. long hair colour

Short hair, especially with a fade, is quick to colour and very forgiving: much of the colour is cut away every few weeks, so regrowth is rarely an issue. Longer hair takes more product and more time, and benefits from more careful maintenance between appointments.

How long does it last?

  • Short hair colour: usually looks its best for three to five weeks, often lining up nicely with your regular haircut schedule.
  • Longer hair colour: roots typically start showing after four to six weeks.
  • Beard colour: beards grow fast and are washed often, so expect two to four weeks.

Caring for coloured hair

  • Use a gentle, sulphate-free shampoo to slow fading.
  • Wash with lukewarm water, not hot.
  • Protect your hair from strong sun in the summer — a cap on the trails or the golf course helps.
  • Moisturize — coloured hair and beards can feel drier, especially in winter.

Before your first colour appointment

  • Be honest about what you want. "Just take the edge off the grey" and "make it all dark brown" are very different goals.
  • Mention any sensitivities. If you've ever reacted to hair dye, a product, or have a sensitive scalp, tell your barber before you start.
  • Bring photos of yourself from a few years ago if that's the look you're after.
