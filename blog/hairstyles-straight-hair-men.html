The Best Hairstyles for Men With Straight Hair

Straight hair takes clean lines beautifully but can look flat without the right cut. Here are the styles that make it work, from buzz cuts to slick backs.

October 6, 20263 min readBlue Shadows Barber Studio

Straight hair has a lot going for it. It's easy to comb, it shows off precise cutting, and it holds clean lines like no other hair type. But it also has a reputation for falling flat, separating at the crown, and looking limp by mid-afternoon. The right haircut — and the right product — turns those weaknesses into strengths.

The principles

  • Create texture. Point cutting and texturizing on top give straight hair movement and stop it from looking flat.
  • Use structure. Straight hair shows clean lines well, so fades, tapers, and sharp parts look especially crisp.
  • Mind the cowlicks. Straight hair makes cowlicks more obvious. A good barber will cut with them, not against them.
  • Choose matte products. They add grip and volume without weighing hair down.

Best haircuts for straight hair

Buzz cut

Straight hair makes for an especially clean buzz cut, with even length and no frizz. Add a fade on the sides and a sharp lineup for a modern finish. It's the ultimate low-maintenance cut.

Crew cut

Slightly longer on top than a buzz, tapered on the sides. Straight hair stands up neatly at this length, making for a tidy, classic look.

Textured crop

A short, choppy crop is one of the best cuts for straight hair, because the texturizing creates movement the hair wouldn't have on its own. Push it forward with a little matte clay.

Side part

Straight hair holds a side part effortlessly. Choose a soft natural part, or a defined part line for extra sharpness. Pair with a low taper for a classic look or a skin fade for contrast.

Slick back

Straight hair lies flat and smooth when slicked back, giving a polished finish. Use pomade for a classic shine, or a matte cream for a more modern look.

Quiff

Straight hair can hold a quiff well with a blow-dry and a strong matte product. Ask your barber to add texture on top so it doesn't look stiff.

Fringe and French crop

A forward-styled fringe falls naturally with straight hair and is great for covering a high forehead or a receding hairline.

Medium-length layered cut

If you want length, add layers to give straight hair shape and movement — otherwise it can hang flat and heavy.

Styling tips for straight hair

  1. Blow-dry for volume. Even 60 seconds of blow-drying in the direction you want makes a huge difference.
  2. Use a pre-styler. A sea salt spray or volumizing spray on damp hair adds grip.
  3. Finish with matte clay or paste — use a small amount, warm it in your hands, and apply back to front.
  4. Avoid heavy oils and creams on fine straight hair; they flatten it quickly.
  5. Don't over-wash. Straight hair gets oily faster, but washing every day can actually make your scalp produce more oil.
