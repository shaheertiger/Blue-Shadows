Men's Haircut Trends for 2026: From Low Tapers to Natural Texture

What are guys actually asking for this year? Here are the haircut trends we're seeing in the chair in 2026, and who each one suits.

October 6, 20263 min readBlue Shadows Barber Studio

Haircut trends move fast online, but what actually shows up in a barber's chair tends to move a little slower — and say more about how people want to look day to day. Here's what we're seeing guys ask for in 2026, and how to make each one work for your hair.

1. The low taper takes over

The high-contrast skin fade isn't going anywhere, but more clients are choosing the softer low taper: short at the sideburns and neckline, with more weight left through the sides. It looks clean and current without the stark contrast — and it grows out gracefully, which matters to anyone who can't visit every two weeks.

Best for: straight, wavy, and curly hair; professionals; anyone who wants low maintenance.

2. Textured crops keep evolving

The textured crop — short, choppy, and pushed forward with a fade underneath — is still one of the most requested cuts. In 2026 it's getting a little longer and softer on top, with more movement and less of a blunt fringe.

Best for: fine to medium hair; round and long faces; anyone who wants style with minimal effort.

3. Natural texture, embraced

More guys with curls and waves are letting them do their thing instead of fighting them. That means cuts designed around natural curl patterns, with shape and definition rather than length removed. Burst and drop fades pair perfectly with curly tops. Our curly hair guide has more ideas.

Best for: wavy, curly, and coily hair.

4. Modern flow and mid-length cuts

Longer hair is having a moment — think tucked-behind-the-ear flow, curtains, and mid-length layered cuts. The modern version is shaped and intentional, with the sides and neckline kept clean so it looks deliberate rather than overgrown.

Best for: thick hair with some natural movement; patient growers.

5. The buzz cut, elevated

The buzz is back as a deliberate style choice, not just a summer fallback. The elevated version adds a skin fade or taper, a sharp lineup, and sometimes colour. It's bold, clean, and almost zero effort.

Best for: strong head shapes; active lifestyles; anyone embracing thinning hair.

6. Classic side parts, softened

The side part never really leaves, but in 2026 it's less slick and more natural — matte products, a soft part rather than a hard shaved line, and a low taper on the sides. It's the go-to for weddings, interviews, and office life.

Best for: straight and wavy hair; oval and long faces; professional settings.

7. Grey, owned or blended

Men are approaching grey hair in two directions: some are embracing it with sharper cuts that make silver look intentional, while others choose subtle grey blending to soften the contrast. Both look great. Read more in our men's hair colour guide and our guide to hairstyles for men over 40.

8. Detailed beard work

Beards are trending shorter and more sculpted — tight, defined cheek lines, a crisp neckline, and a smooth blend into the haircut. Straight-razor detailing is the finishing touch that separates a good beard from a great one.

Trends vs. what suits you

The best haircut is rarely the most trendy one — it's the one that works with your face shape, hair type, and lifestyle. Use trends as inspiration, then let your barber adapt them. Our face shape guide is a good place to start.
