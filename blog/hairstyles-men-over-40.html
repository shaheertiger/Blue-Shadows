Best Hairstyles for Men Over 40: Barber-Recommended Cuts

Your hair changes after 40 — thinning, greying, a hairline on the move. The right cut works with those changes instead of fighting them.

October 6, 20263 min readBlue Shadows Barber Studio

Somewhere around 40, most men notice their hair changing. It might be a little thinner at the crown, a hairline that's moved back, more grey at the temples, or hair that just doesn't behave like it used to. None of that means you can't look great. It means the haircut that worked at 25 probably isn't the one that works best now.

Here's what we recommend to clients in their 40s, 50s, and beyond.

The principles

  • Shorter is usually sharper. Shorter cuts look intentional and minimize thinning.
  • Work with texture. Texture on top creates the look of fullness; flat, combed-over hair highlights thin spots.
  • Keep the edges clean. A crisp neckline and tidy ears make any cut look fresh and polished.
  • Grooming matters more. Eyebrows, ear and nose hair, and beard lines all make a bigger difference with age.

Best haircuts for men over 40

The classic taper

A taper with a couple of inches on top, styled to the side or back. Timeless, professional, and incredibly versatile. It works with thick or thinning hair and looks good with grey.

Short textured crop

One of the best cuts for a receding hairline. A short, textured fringe pushed slightly forward softens the hairline and creates the look of density. Pair with a low or mid fade.

Crew cut

Short on the sides, slightly longer on top, and easy to maintain. The crew cut is a classic for men with thinning hair — it looks clean and confident with zero effort.

The silver side part

If you're going grey, a sharp side part with a low taper makes silver hair look distinguished and intentional rather than accidental.

The buzz with a fade

For significant thinning or a receding hairline, going short is often the most confident move. A buzz with a fade and a sharp lineup looks strong and deliberate.

Slick back (for thick hair)

If you've kept your density, a slicked-back style with a low fade is polished and grown-up. Use a matte or low-shine product to keep it modern.

Working with thinning hair

  • Don't grow it long to compensate. Longer hair separates and shows more scalp.
  • Avoid comb-overs. They draw attention to exactly what you're hiding.
  • Use matte products. Shine reflects light and makes the scalp more visible. Matte clays add texture and fullness. See our hair products guide.
  • Keep the sides shorter. When the sides are tighter, the top looks fuller by comparison.

Grey: embrace it or blend it

Both are great options. Some men love the distinguished look of natural grey, especially with a sharp, structured cut. Others prefer to soften it with grey blending, which reduces contrast without the obvious look of full colour. Learn more in our men's hair colour guide.

Beards over 40

A well-kept beard can add structure to the jawline and balance a receding hairline. Keep it tidy and defined — longer, unshaped beards tend to add years. If your beard is greying unevenly, a beard colour or blend can even it out.

Grooming details

The small things add up. Regular eyebrow tidying, trimming ear and nose hair, and a defined cheek line make a bigger difference after 40 than almost anything else. Our Thread Detail ($10) cleans up eyebrows and cheeks, and a Steam Finish hot towel ($5) is a great way to end any appointment.
