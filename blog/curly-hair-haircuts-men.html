Cool Haircuts for Men With Curly Hair: Embrace the Curls

Curly hair isn't something to fight — it's something to shape. Here are the best cuts for curls, waves, and coils, plus a simple routine to keep them defined.

October 6, 20263 min readBlue Shadows Barber Studio

For years, a lot of guys with curly hair just kept it short to avoid dealing with it. Today, more men are embracing their natural texture — and the right cut makes curls look intentional, defined, and incredibly sharp. The key is choosing a shape that works with your curl pattern rather than against it.

Know your curl type

Curly hair covers a huge range:

  • Wavy: loose S-shaped waves that can go flat or frizzy.
  • Curly: defined ringlets or spirals with more volume.
  • Coily: tight, springy coils with lots of density and shrinkage.

Each type behaves differently when cut, dries differently, and needs different products. A good barber will look at how your hair curls before deciding how to cut it — and often cut curly hair dry, or partly dry, to see the true shape.

Best haircuts for curly hair

Burst fade with curly top

The burst fade curves around the ear and leaves length at the back, creating a rounded shape that suits curls perfectly. One of the most popular curly cuts right now. Learn more in our fade guide.

Curly crop with a taper or drop fade

A shorter, defined curly top with a low taper or drop fade. Clean, modern, and easy to maintain — curls do most of the styling.

High top

For coily hair, a high top with tight sides creates a bold, structured silhouette. Precision shaping on top is everything here.

Medium-length curls

Letting curls grow to a medium length shows off their full pattern. Keep the sides and neckline cleaned up and remove bulk so the shape stays balanced, not triangular.

Short sides, longer curly fringe

Curls falling forward over the forehead with a mid fade underneath. Relaxed and stylish, and great for softening a strong forehead.

Twists and textured styles

For coily hair, twists or sponge-textured tops paired with a fade or taper look sharp and are easy to maintain.

A simple curl care routine

  1. Shampoo less. Curly hair is naturally drier. Shampoo two or three times a week at most, with a sulphate-free formula.
  2. Condition every time. Conditioner is the most important product for curls. Leave it in for a few minutes.
  3. Apply product to wet hair. A curl cream or leave-in conditioner on soaking-wet hair locks in definition.
  4. Don't rub with a towel. Scrunch gently with a microfibre towel or cotton T-shirt to reduce frizz.
  5. Air dry or diffuse. Avoid touching your curls while they dry — it causes frizz.
  6. Refresh with water. On day two or three, a spray of water and a little cream brings curls back.

For more on products, see our men's hair product guide.

Curls through the seasons

Ontario weather affects curls more than any other hair type. Humid summers cause frizz; dry winters cause brittleness and static. Use lighter, anti-frizz products in summer and richer moisturizing products in winter — and protect curls under a toque with a silk or satin-lined hat if you can.
