Best Haircuts for Men With Round or Chubby Faces

The right haircut can make a round face look sharper and more defined in seconds. It's all about height on top, tight sides, and a few clean angles.

October 6, 20263 min readBlue Shadows Barber Studio

If your face is about as wide as it is long, with soft curves at the jaw and cheeks, you have a round face. Fuller faces fall into the same category. The good news: with the right cut, a round face can look sharp, structured, and defined — and the changes that make the biggest difference are simple.

The principles

  • Add height on top. Volume above the forehead makes the face look longer and slimmer.
  • Keep the sides tight. Short sides — especially a mid or high fade — remove width.
  • Create angles. Defined lines, side parts, and sharp edges balance soft curves.
  • Avoid weight at the cheeks. Hair that sits wide or falls around the ears adds roundness.

Best haircuts for round faces

High fade with a textured quiff

Tight, high-faded sides with a lifted, textured top. The contrast between the narrow sides and the height on top is the single most effective way to elongate a round face.

Pompadour with a mid fade

A swept-back pompadour adds height and a strong vertical line. Pair it with a mid fade to keep the sides slim.

Angular side part

A side part with a defined line (natural or lightly shaved) adds an angle that breaks up the roundness. Keep the sides short with a low or mid taper.

Faux hawk

Short sides with a strip of longer, textured hair through the centre, pushed up. It draws the eye upward and adds an edge to the look.

Textured crop with lift

A crop can work for round faces if it has height. Ask for extra length on top with choppy texture styled upward rather than flat and forward, and pair it with a high fade.

Spiky or messy textured top

Short, piece-y texture styled up with matte clay adds height and angles with very little effort.

Styles to approach carefully

  • Blunt, straight fringes — cut across the forehead and make the face look shorter and wider.
  • Bowl cuts and rounded shapes — echo the shape of the face.
  • Long, heavy sides — add width exactly where you don't want it.
  • Uniform buzz cuts — can work, but add a fade and sharp lineup to create shape.

Beards: your secret weapon

For round and fuller faces, a well-shaped beard can do as much as the haircut:

  • Keep the sides short and let the chin grow slightly longer — this elongates the face.
  • Define the cheek line and neckline sharply. A crisp jawline line creates structure and definition.
  • Try a goatee, anchor, or short boxed beard with a pointed chin for added length.
  • Avoid very full, round beards that add width at the sides.

A sharp neckline is especially important — it creates the visual jawline that makes the whole face look more defined.

Styling tips

  • Use a matte clay or paste to build height that lasts all day. See our guide to men's hair products.
  • Blow-dry the top upward before applying product for extra volume.
  • Keep up with regular cuts — overgrown sides are the fastest way to lose the effect.
